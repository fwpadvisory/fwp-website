Pointing your domain
to the new website

What needs to change at your domain provider — and what stays untouched.

Prepared by The Content Counsel  ·  thecontentcounsel.com

The new website is built and running. The last step is to point fwpadvisory.com.au at it, so visitors reach the new site instead of the current one.

The one thing we must not break

Your email must not be touched. Your email runs on Google Workspace, but the records that control it live in the same place as your website records — currently at Wix. If the domain's nameservers are switched over wholesale, email stops working immediately, along with your spam protection (SPF) and Google verification.

So we are deliberately not moving your DNS. We change only the two records that control the website, and leave everything else exactly as it is.

Step by step

Step 1 — We add the domain on our side

We add fwpadvisory.com.au and www.fwpadvisory.com.au to the hosting project. The system then displays the two exact records that need to be entered. We will send you those values.

They are normally:

TypeName / HostValue
A@76.76.21.21
CNAMEwwwcname.vercel-dns.com
Always use the values shown on screen at the time rather than these — they can change.

Step 2 — You update the two records at Wix

  1. Sign in to Wix and open Domains → fwpadvisory.com.au.
  2. Open DNS Records (sometimes shown as “Advanced DNS”).
  3. Find the A records for @. There are currently three, all beginning 185.230.63. — remove them and add the single A record we send you.
  4. Find the CNAME for www (currently cdn1.wixdns.net) and replace its value with the one we send you.
  5. Save.
Do not change anything else on that page — in particular the MX and TXT records. Those are your email.

If Wix will not let you edit the records because the domain is still connected to your old Wix site, choose Disconnect site first. The domain stays in your Wix account and keeps working; only the link to the old site is removed.

Step 3 — Wait a short while

The change usually takes effect within 15 minutes to 2 hours. The security certificate (the padlock in the browser) is issued automatically once the records are visible — nothing to buy or install.

What we do straight afterwards

Once the domain resolves to the new site, we complete the remaining technical work:

  • Point the site's internal addresses and sitemap at the new domain.
  • Re-authorise the content editor (Sanity Studio) for the new domain.
  • Update the BOMA guide form: add the domain to its allowed list and repoint the post-submission page. Without this the free-guide form stops working on the new domain, so it is done immediately.
  • Set up Google Search Console for the domain and submit the sitemap.
  • Re-test the whole site on desktop and mobile.

Good to know

  • Timing. Pick a quiet period rather than a busy weekday morning. There is no outage as such, but visitors move across gradually as the change propagates.
  • Reversible. If anything looks wrong, putting the original A and CNAME values back restores the old site.
  • Your old Wix site. Once the domain points to the new website, the Wix site will no longer be reachable at this address. Keep a copy of anything you still need from it.
  • Email, again. Because we never touch MX or TXT, your email keeps running throughout with no interruption.
