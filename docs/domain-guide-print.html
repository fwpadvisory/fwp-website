To make fwpadvisory.com.au show the new website, two records need to be updated at Wix. We will send you the exact values to paste in.

Please don't change anything else. Your email runs on Google Workspace and is controlled from the same page. As long as the MX and TXT records are left alone, your email keeps working with no interruption.

The two records

TypeNameNew value
A@76.76.21.21
CNAMEwwwcname.vercel-dns.com

Steps in Wix

  1. Sign in to Wix and open Domains → fwpadvisory.com.au.
  2. Open DNS Records.
  3. Under A records you'll see three entries starting with 185.230.63. — delete them and add the single A record above.
  4. Under CNAME, find www and change its value to the one above.
  5. Save.

If Wix won't let you edit the records, choose Disconnect site first. Your domain stays in your Wix account.

Then

Let us know once it's saved. The change takes effect within 15 minutes to 2 hours, the padlock (security certificate) appears automatically, and we finish the rest from our side.
