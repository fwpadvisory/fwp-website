# Adding and editing articles — a short guide

Your website articles live in a content editor called **Sanity Studio**. You can write,
edit and publish articles yourself — no developer needed, and nothing in the website
code has to change.

---

## 1. Opening the editor

Go to:

**https://fwpadvisory.com.au/studio**

(While the site is still on its temporary address, use
`https://fwp-website-mu.vercel.app/studio`.)

Sign in with the Google account you used to create the Sanity project. You only need to
do this once per device.

---

## 2. Writing a new article

1. In the left-hand list, click **Article**.
2. Click the **+** (new document) button at the top.
3. Fill in the fields (see the next section).
4. Click **Publish** (bottom right) when you are ready for it to go on the website.

Your article appears at the top of **Latest Articles** on the Resources page —
newest first, automatically. You never need to sort or categorise anything.

---

## 3. What each field does

| Field | What to put in it |
|---|---|
| **Title** | The article headline. |
| **Slug** | The web address. Click **Generate** and it fills itself in from the title. |
| **Category** | Optional. Leave blank if you like — it isn't shown on the website. |
| **Excerpt / summary** | 1–2 sentences. This is the text shown on the article card. |
| **Author** | Optional. Leave blank and it shows "Family Wealth Protection Advisory". |
| **Body** | The article itself. See "Formatting" below. |
| **Featured image** | The picture on the card and at the top of the article. |
| **Disclaimer** | Your general-information disclaimer, shown in a box at the foot of the article. |
| **Source note** | Optional. Case references or sources, shown in small text at the very bottom. |
| **SEO title / SEO description** | Optional. Only fill these in if you want Google to show something different from the title and summary. |
| **Publication date** | The date shown on the article and used to order the list. Set it when you publish. |

---

## 4. Formatting the body

The **Body** field works like a simple word processor.

- Type normally for ordinary paragraphs.
- To make a **heading**, highlight the line and choose **H2** from the style dropdown
  (the box that says "Normal").
- To make a **bullet list**, click the bullet-list icon in the toolbar.
- To make text **bold** or *italic*, highlight it and use the toolbar buttons.

Keep headings as **H2** — the website styles them to match the rest of the site.

---

## 5. Adding a featured image

1. Scroll to **Featured image**.
2. Click **Upload** and choose a picture from your computer.
3. After it uploads you can drag the small circle on the image to choose which part
   stays visible when the picture is cropped on smaller screens.

Landscape pictures work best. Anything roughly 1200–1600 pixels wide is plenty.

---

## 6. Drafts, editing and unpublishing

- **Save a draft** — just stop typing. Sanity saves automatically as you go, and the
  document stays a draft until you press **Publish**. Drafts are never visible on the
  website.
- **Edit a published article** — open it, make your changes, then press **Publish**
  again to push the update live.
- **Take an article down** — open it, click the **⋮** menu at the bottom and choose
  **Unpublish**. It disappears from the website but stays in the editor.
- **Undo** — the **⋮** menu also has document history, so you can go back to an
  earlier version.

---

## 7. When do changes appear on the website?

The website is a fast static site, which means it is rebuilt after content changes
rather than reading the database on every visit.

- **Right now:** after you press Publish, let us know and we will refresh the site.
- **Once automatic publishing is switched on** (this is waiting on one outstanding
  technical item), your changes will appear on their own within a few minutes.

If you have published something and don't see it on the site yet, that is why — the
content is safe, the site just needs its refresh.

---

## 8. A few tips

- Write the article in the editor rather than pasting from Word — pasted text
  sometimes brings hidden formatting with it. If you do paste, use
  **Paste and match style** (Cmd+Shift+V).
- Always fill in the **Excerpt** — it is what people read on the card before clicking.
- Keep the **Disclaimer** on every article for consistency.
- Nothing you do in the editor can break the website design. The worst case is an
  article that needs tidying up.
